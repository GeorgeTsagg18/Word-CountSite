2026 Social Media Character Limits Guide

Social media platforms have strict guidelines. Posting a truncated message can ruin your engagement and professional image. Our built-in social media character counter features live progress bars for the most popular platforms in 2026:

  • X (formerly Twitter): The standard limit remains 280 characters for regular users. Our live tracker ensures you never waste time writing a tweet that gets cut off.
  • LinkedIn: The platform allows up to 3,000 characters for organic posts. Since LinkedIn is built for professional storytelling, using a character counter helps you maximize your thought leadership content.
  • Instagram: Captions can be up to 2,200 characters long. However, since Instagram truncates captions after 125 characters in the feed, our tool helps you plan your "hook" carefully while keeping track of the total length.
  • TikTok: The video platform allows up to 2,200 characters for descriptions, giving creators ample room for SEO keywords and hashtags.

Optimize Your Content with Keyword Density Analysis

Writing for the web means balancing readability with SEO. If you overstuff keywords, search engines might penalize your page. If you use too few, you might not rank at all. Our built-in keyword density checker analyzes your text in real-time. It filters out common stop words (like "the", "and", "is") and highlights the top 5 most frequently used terms. This allows copywriters and content marketers to see exactly which topics dominate their text, ensuring optimal alignment with target search queries.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I count words in a text block?

Simply type or paste your text into the main editor area. The tool will automatically calculate the total number of words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in real-time.

Is this character counter completely free?

Yes! CharacterCap is a 100% free utility tool. There are no premium plans, no hidden fees, and no account registrations required.

Does the tool save my work?

Yes, we use your browser's local storage to auto-save your text. If you accidentally close the tab, your text will be waiting for you when you return. However, we never upload your data to any external servers.

What is Zen Mode?

Writing requires focus. Zen Mode provides a completely distraction-free environment by hiding all menus, sidebars, and metrics, leaving only your text on the screen. It is perfect for getting your initial thoughts down before you start editing.

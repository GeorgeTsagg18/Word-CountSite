Frequently Asked Questions

Why use a dedicated word counter online?

While traditional word processors can count words, a dedicated web tool offers advanced features you won't find anywhere else. From tracking keyword density to calculating reading and speaking times, it is built specifically to improve the flow and SEO value of your content instantly.

How do I clean up messy text?

If you have ever copied text from a PDF, you know it often comes with broken lines and awkward spacing. Just paste it into the editor and use our one-click "Remove Extra Spaces" and "Remove Line Breaks" buttons to instantly format your document and make it readable.

Is the social media limit counter accurate?

Yes. We track the character limits exactly as major platforms do. The live progress bars turn red the moment you exceed the limit, ensuring your carefully crafted captions never get cut off mid-sentence.

What is Zen Mode?

Writing requires focus. Zen Mode provides a completely distraction-free environment by hiding all menus, sidebars, and metrics, leaving only your text on the screen. It is perfect for getting your initial thoughts down before you start editing.

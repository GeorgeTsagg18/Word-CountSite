Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

CharacterCap is a client-side tool. This means that any text you type or paste into our editor is processed entirely within your web browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit your typed documents to our servers.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads on our website to keep our tool free. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.

3. Local Storage

Our website uses your browser's Local Storage to auto-save your text and remember your Dark Mode preference. This data remains on your device and is not accessible by us.
